Recommendations
P3 Ship P3-A (pill badge) or P3-B (phantom plus). A = shape teaches; B = typographically purest. Both low effort.
P4 P4-B (filled bar) as the general implementation — handles decimals + wide ranges. P4-A (spaced dots) as a config-driven variant for small integer habits. Medium effort.
P5 P5-B (tap to focus, then drag) — teaches itself through use, no hint infrastructure, merges tap and scrub. If too complex, ship P5-A as interim. P5-B: medium effort. P5-A: low-medium.
