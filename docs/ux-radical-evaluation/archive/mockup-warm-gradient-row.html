Design note — the paper quality

The gradient is directional — it warms from the left edge and fades rightward into the page. This mimics the way a fountain pen lays down more ink at stroke entry than exit: heaviest at the mark point, dissolving into the surface. The effect is environmental rather than iconographic. It does not announce itself; it settles.

The warmed divider below done rows creates a subtle boundary between marked and unmarked territory — like a soft colour field shift in a watercolour wash. The unmarked rows above and below remain neutral.
