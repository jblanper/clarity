Design note — the most radical read

This proposal removes all widget affordance. There is nothing to "press" visually — the row is the control, the highlight is the receipt. This is the closest digital translation of marking a Hobonichi page: you press the whole field, not a checkbox.

The risk: users who have never seen the app may not discover that the rows are tappable. For Clarity, where the interaction is a daily ritual rather than an onboarding task, this risk is low. The gesture is learned once and becomes invisible — which is exactly what calm technology aims for.
