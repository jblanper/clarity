# Calm & Bold Research for Clarity

## Calm Technology Principles (Amber Case)
- **Minimal attention:** Technology should require the least amount of attention. It should inform without taking focus.
- **Center vs. Periphery:** A calm tool moves from the periphery of attention to the center when needed, then back.
- **Amplifying Humanity:** It should help you be more human, not more machine-like.

## Zen Philosophy & 'Ma'
- **Shoshin (Beginner's Mind):** Approaching habits without the weight of past "failure" or "success." Digital translation: A "Fresh Start" mode that resets visual cues while keeping data safe.
- **Wabi-Sabi:** Finding beauty in imperfection and transience. Digital translation: UI elements that "age" or show subtle wear based on usage, celebrating the human reality of missed days.
- **Ma (Negative Space):** The space between things is as important as the things themselves. Digital translation: Deliberate "Pause" screens or empty states that encourage breathing rather than doing.

## Solarpunk & Permacomputing (100 Rabbits - 100r.co)
- **Low-Energy Design:** Tools should be resilient and run on minimal hardware/power. Digital translation: Aggressive static export (SSG) and zero-dependency local storage.
- **Tool Repairability:** Users should understand and own their tools. Digital translation: Plain-text JSON exports that anyone can read without the app.
- **Ecosystem Thinking:** Software as part of a life-cycle. Digital translation: Habits that evolve or "hibernate" with the seasons, rather than remaining static and rigid.
- **Resilient UI:** Aesthetics that don't rely on modern, high-bandwidth "flash." Typographic-first design is a form of resilience.

## Analog Inspiration Patterns
- **Morning Pages (Julia Cameron):** Three pages of stream-of-consciousness writing. Digital translation: A mode that hides what you've typed as you type it to prevent editing.
- **Bullet Journaling (Ryder Carroll):** Rapid logging and "migration" (moving tasks forward manually). Digital translation: Forcing manual "re-entry" of uncompleted habits to confirm their importance.
- **Commonplace Books:** A central place for ideas, quotes, and reflections. Digital translation: Linking "Moments" to specific reflections for better discovery.

## Bold Ideas for Minimalist UIs
- **Seasonal Typography:** Change the font weight or tracking based on the season (e.g., wider, lighter in summer; denser, bolder in winter) to reflect natural rhythms.
- **"The Archive as Garden":** Old data isn't "history," it's "compost." Faded text for old reflections, growing darker as they are revisited.
- **Intentional Obfuscation:** A "Blur Mode" for the history view, where you only see the "texture" of your months until you deliberately hover over a specific day.
- **Local-Only Sync via QR:** Sync between phone and laptop by simply showing a QR code of the `localStorage` JSON—no cloud, no accounts.
